# Hierarchical-NURBS-Inspired-Multi-Domain-Loss-for-Training-Deep-Time-Series-Forecasting-Models

### Abstract

Time-series forecasting is a technique with applications in fields ranging from finance to transportation. Traditional loss functions, such as Mean Squared Error (MSE) and Mean Absolute Error (MAE) aim to minimize point-wise deviations between predicted and true values. However, these functions often fall short when capturing the periodic and spectral characteristics inherent in many time-series datasets. In this paper, we introduce Hierarchical NURBS-Inspired Multi-Domain Loss, a novel loss function that, inspired by Non-Uniform Rational Basis Splines (NURBS), leverages weighted basis splines to form hierarchical time-series representations, which are then used in a loss function in spanning several domains(e.g: time domain, frequency domain). Our experimental results show that not only allows models to surpass their differently-trained counterparts but also improves the scaling laws of time-series forecasting models altogether. Notably, a 3-layer MLP trained with \methodName surpasses all previous models trained with MSE on 29 of 90 time-series forecasting tasks across 9 datasets.
